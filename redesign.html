Redesign questions, answered

Will I lose my Google rankings?

We keep your domain, carry over the content that ranks, and redirect old page addresses to the new ones so Google can follow the move. Rankings can wobble for a few weeks after any redesign, and we'll keep an eye on it with you.

Do I keep my domain and email?

Yes. Your domain and email stay exactly as they are. We only change the website.

What happens to my old website?

It stays live until you approve the new one. Then we switch over and keep a backup of the old site.

What if I don't like the redesign?

Tell us what feels off and we'll rework it. You only pay once you've approved the final site.

Who owns the new website?

You do. If you ever move on, the site goes with you.

Where are you based?

Our team is in India. That's how we keep prices fair and get sites live in a week, and we're happy to talk on a call at a time that suits you.
